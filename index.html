Course outcomes

Approved outcomes from the syllabus (the syllabus document itself: To be added).

  • CO1: To understand the basics of data warehousing and mining.
  • CO2: To learn the data preprocessing, language, architectures, concept description.
  • CO3: To learn the association rules and its algorithms.
  • CO4: To learn the classification and clustering rules and the respective algorithms.

Practice task

Build and explain a multidimensional data model (CO1)

Work in pairs, based on the Week 1 and Week 2 activities.

  1. Sketch a multidimensional data model for a scenario of your choice. Scenario supplied by faculty: To be added
  2. Draw and label the data warehouse architecture that would hold your model, using English technical terms.
  3. Give a short peer teach-back: explain your model in Tamil, keeping the English technical terms.

Evidence: peer teach-back and group diagram. Marking criteria: To be added
